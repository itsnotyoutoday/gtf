# What is gtf

gtf is a MUD client. It reads and runs TinyFugue scripts, so most `.tf` files, worlds, macros and triggers written for TinyFugue work here, but gtf is its own program: it is not TinyFugue and is not made by the TinyFugue authors. See [[gtf and TinyFugue]].

## Where to start

* [[Squatter]]: keep your connections up and attach SquatClient (the desktop and phone app) to them. Three commands: `/squatter on`, `/pair`, then add the device in SquatClient. See [[Squatter quick start]].
* `/help topics` lists every topic written for gtf.
* [[gtf and TinyFugue]]: what "TinyFugue-compatible" means here, and the bundled TinyFugue manual.
* `/help copyright`: who wrote what, and the license.

Anything `/help` cannot find among gtf's own topics is looked up in the bundled TinyFugue manual (it says so when it does), because the commands and the scripting language are shared. `/help tf <topic>` looks only there.
