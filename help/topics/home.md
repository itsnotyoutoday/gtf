# Home

Welcome to the gtf wiki!

**gtf** is a terminal client for MUDs and other text games. It reads and runs TinyFugue scripts, so most `.tf` files, worlds, macros and triggers written for TinyFugue work in it, but gtf is its own program: it is TinyFugue-compatible, it is not TinyFugue, and it is not made by the TinyFugue authors (see [[gtf and TinyFugue]]). On top of the familiar screen it adds panels and graphics driven by what the game sends, a console protocol for space-game consoles, restarts that keep your connections, and a remote-access server so you can carry your session to a phone.

These pages are the same documentation you get inside gtf with `/help`. They are written once, in the repository, and published here.
